## ELEVON
t_r:he_1:20,t_s:p_1:50:t_t:x:50:m_r:he_1:20

in this case, for the operation (before the first `:`), we have "t_" or "m_" at the beginning for `transform` or `morph`, then we have `r`, `s` or `t` for "rotate", "scale" or "translate" respectively then we have the reference `he_1` for "hinge-line elevon 1", `p_1` for "point 1", `x` for "x axis"
and finally the amount of movement: 20 degrees for rotation, 50x for scale, 50 mm for translation
